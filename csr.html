Impact Model

From intention to accountable action.

A strong CSR program needs more than goodwill. It needs clear priorities, transparent selection, documented delivery and ongoing learning.

Listen

Understand the real needs of communities and potential beneficiaries.

Plan

Define the objective, target group, resources and intended outcome.

Deliver

Provide support with dignity, fairness and responsible stewardship.

Review

Document outcomes and improve future initiatives based on experience.
